Photography has been a passion of mine for several years now. In this post, I want to share how I got started and what I've learned along the way.

## How It Started

It all began with a simple point-and-shoot camera during a family vacation. I was fascinated by how a single moment could be captured and preserved forever.

## Learning the Craft

Over time, I've learned about:

- **Composition:** The rule of thirds, leading lines, and framing
- **Lighting:** Natural light, golden hour, and shadows
- **Post-processing:** Basic editing in Lightroom and Photoshop
- **Equipment:** Understanding cameras, lenses, and accessories

## Favorite Subjects

I especially enjoy photographing:

- Landscapes and nature scenes
- Urban architecture
- Street photography
- Portrait photography

Check out my [gallery page](/gallery) to see some of my favorite shots!

## Final Thoughts

Photography has taught me to see the world differently and appreciate the beauty in everyday moments. Whether you're a beginner or experienced photographer, keep shooting and experimenting!
